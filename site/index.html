Tasks, projects, goals and a Pomodoro timer, on top of Microsoft To Do.

  • Your tasks stay in your own Microsoft account. They show up in the To Do apps too.
  • Group work into projects and set goals by repetitions, time or quantity.
  • Log what each session produced, with evidence, and watch progress add up.
  • Focus with a Pomodoro timer that logs minutes toward time goals.

Works with personal, work and school Microsoft accounts. The demo keeps data in this browser only.
